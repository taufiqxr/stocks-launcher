Stocks Launcher — Privacy policy

Effective 2026-07-31

What we collect

Nothing. Stocks Launcher has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you type leaves your browser except the pages you choose to open.

What is stored

Your site picks (which research sites open) are saved in Chrome's own extension storage, synced by Chrome across your signed-in browsers. The ticker dataset ships inside the extension. Nothing else is stored.

Network requests

The extension makes exactly one kind of network request of its own: it fetches each research site's public favicon (via Google's favicon service) to draw the site picker. No ticker, query, or personal information is sent anywhere by the extension itself.
